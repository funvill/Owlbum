OpenStreetMap

Owlbum's Map view can show map tiles from OpenStreetMap
(https://www.openstreetmap.org). It asks before it loads any, and loads
none while the setting "Map: offline" is chosen. The tiles are downloaded
from https://tile.openstreetmap.org when the map is shown; none are
bundled with Owlbum.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors. The data is available under the
Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0, https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright.
The map shows the credit "© OpenStreetMap contributors" in its corner
while tiles are loaded.

Owlbum uses the tiles under the OpenStreetMap Foundation's tile usage
policy, https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/tiles/: a person
looking at their own photos, with no bulk downloading of tiles.
