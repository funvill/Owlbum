Natural Earth

Owlbum's country outlines (crates/owlbum-core/data/countries.bin), used for
the Country row and the country search, are made from Natural Earth's
1:10m Admin 0 - Countries, version 5.1.2 (https://www.naturalearthdata.com),
simplified by scripts/countries.mjs.

Natural Earth data is in the public domain. No permission is needed to use
it. Made with Natural Earth. Free vector and raster map data @
naturalearthdata.com.

Borders follow Natural Earth's default view, which shows the side that
administers an area on the ground. Owlbum takes no position on any dispute.
