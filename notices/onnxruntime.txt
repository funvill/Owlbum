ONNX Runtime

Owlbum's photo tagging runs its models on Microsoft ONNX Runtime 1.31.1
(https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime), shipped as onnxruntime.dll from
the NuGet package Microsoft.ML.OnnxRuntime 1.31.1, unmodified. ONNX Runtime
