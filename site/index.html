Owlbum

A photo browser for the folders you already have. Your photos stay where they are, and nothing about them leaves your computer.

  • Star and hide. Keep the good ones in front and the rest out of the way, without moving or deleting a file.
  • Find by what is in a photo. Optional tagging runs on your computer and lets you search for a dog, a beach or a birthday cake.
  • Simple edits. Rotate, crop, straighten and adjust light and colour. The original file is never changed.
  • Drag into other programs. Drag a photo straight into an email, a chat or an editor.

Privacy

Everything stays on this computer. Owlbum uses the network only for OpenStreetMap map tiles, and only after you choose Load maps on the Map tab; to download photo-tagging models from Hugging Face when you ask for one in Settings; and to check GitHub for a new version once a day, unless you turn that off in Help.

No account. No telemetry. The Windows installer may download Microsoft's WebView2 if your computer does not have it.

This page has no cookies, no scripts and no analytics, and it loads nothing from any other site.
